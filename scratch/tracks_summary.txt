### Consumer Products & Payments



#### Income Share Agreements as a Consumer Product



Fund a friend's course, career move, or creative project in exchange for a programmatic share of future earnings, settled automatically onstream.



- [$ALEX "Human IPO"](https://www.theblock.co/post/61699/this-crypto-entrepreneur-just-sold-20000-worth-of-personal-tokens-on-ethereum): $20K of personal tokens for 15% of income over 3 years, capped at $100K, quarterly DAI dividends. The trust gap in income reporting is what onstream settlement fixes.

- [Superfluid Distributions](https://docs.superfluid.org/docs/concepts/superfluid): One-to-many pools with continuous streaming to members.

- [Sablier (stream-as-NFT)](https://sablier.com): Streams are transferable ERC-721s usable as collateral.



#### Programmable Gifts That Unlock on Conditions



"Here's $500 when you graduate", "here's your share when the company exits": financial commitments that execute automatically without anyone having to remember or chase.



- [Chainlink Programmable Money](https://chain.link/article/programmable-money-mechanics): Escrow and release handled by the contract, executing only when predefined criteria are met.

- [Chainlink Automation tutorial](https://chain.link/tutorials/trigger-smart-contract-execution): Trigger smart contract execution. Automation not confirmed live on Monad; fallback is a self-run keeper bot (see Ride or Die).

### Trust, Identity & AI Infrastructure



#### Expertise Licensing Marketplace



Domain experts license their decision-making patterns to train specialised models, compensated per training run, not per data upload.



- [Bittensor Subnet 52 (Dojo)](https://www.aicoin.com/en/article/447570): The biggest live experiment in paying for AI contributions, with Dojo specifically incentivizing data annotation.

- [Bittensor critical analysis](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.02951v1): Academic analysis of the incentive design.

- [Reward-distribution skew](https://sandeepchinchali.medium.com/decentralized-ai-and-bittensor-an-academics-view-b0ad811d2728): On Bittensor, whale validators earn ~$40K/day while the best-performing miner ranks 66th at ~$940/day. Token-incentive networks tend to pay capital, not contributors.



#### Data Labelling Networks for Physical AI



Contributors earn stablecoin rewards for annotating robotics and sensor data, with onchain micropayments making global participation practical at scale.



- [Bittensor subnets](https://bittensor.ai/subnets): The direct precedent: distributed annotation where miners tag data and validators score accuracy using semantic embeddings. Steal the validator-scoring design — quality control is the whole game in labeling.
